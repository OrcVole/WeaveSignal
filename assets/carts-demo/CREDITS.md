# Cart sound credits

The default cart pack: 50 sounds for the cart wall, chosen on 2026-11-21 to replace the first pack of 2026-10-25 (only its two applause files are kept). Every file is CC0, public domain or CC BY, and each licence was read from the source's own page for that one file on 2026-11-21; nothing CC BY-NC, ND or SA is used. Files marked CC BY need the credit shown here and in `NOTICES.md`.

Each sound was cut, loudness-set to -16 LUFS integrated with its true peak at or below -1 dBTP, and encoded to Ogg Opus at 96 kbit/s, 48 kHz (`ffmpeg`, libopus). A few sounds whose peaks stand far above their body (the clock ticks, the tape stopping, the phone put down, two short hits) sit between -16.5 and -22.5 LUFS rather than be crushed by the limiter. Freesound gives its full-quality originals only to signed-in users, so the Freesound sounds were taken from Freesound's public high-quality Ogg Vorbis preview of each file (about 180 to 280 kbit/s), linked as *Preview* below. The originals and previews are not kept here.
