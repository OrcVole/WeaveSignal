# Cart sound credits

Demo sounds for the cart wall. Every file is CC0, public domain or CC BY, taken from the source's own metadata (Wikimedia Commons file pages through its API, kenney.nl and opengameart.org pages). Files marked CC BY need the credit shown here and in `NOTICES.md`. Each file was cut, loudness-set to about -16 LUFS with peaks below -1 dBTP, and encoded to Ogg Opus 96 kbit/s at 48 kHz; the originals are not kept.

Public-domain files on Commons are as the uploader tagged them; the two US Air Force recordings are US federal government works, and the BBC time-signal pips are a simple tone sequence that Commons tags public domain. The sad-trombone, boing, phone and laugh files are CC BY.
