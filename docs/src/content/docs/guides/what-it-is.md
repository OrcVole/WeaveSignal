WeaveSignal is open-source software for an internet radio station that runs on your own server, with a live studio built in.

It is one installation that gives you a room where people can talk live, the desk that mixes them on air, the automation that plays music and recorded shows between broadcasts, the stream that listeners hear, a chat, and the cards and podcast feeds for your shows.

You host it yourself on a server you control, so the station, its listeners and its recordings are yours.

The studio is for a presenter, a guest sent a link, or a caller who rings in and is screened before the air.
A desk mixes everyone, so listeners hear one clear programme; between shows the station plays music or recorded shows on its own, so listeners always hear something.

## If it does not work

- **You were expecting a website to sign up to.** WeaveSignal is software: someone runs it on their own server, and you use their station.
- **You want to try it first.** The [sandbox desk](https://demo.weavesignal.top/) runs in your browser, with a pretend station, Kettle FM.
