When you finish, your show will be set up and you will be waiting off air for the handover, without touching the show that is on air now.

Your studio app opens in **Preparing** when your show is not the one on air.
A preparing window shows the live desk, so you can follow the show, but nothing in it changes what is on air: its desk buttons and keys are off.
The **Schedule** tab and **Settings** work as usual.

## Set up your show

The first time you sign in as a presenter, the station asks three short parts, which it keeps as the defaults for you and your show:

1. **About you**: your on-air name and a contact for taking a call of yours down.
2. **Your show**: its name, its usual day and time, and links to your channels.
3. **Done**: where these answers now live, so you can change them.

You can run this again at any time from **Settings** › **Your show** › **Set up your show…**.

## Make sure your show is scheduled

Your show is on the schedule with its usual day and time, so the station moves on to it at the post by itself.
If it is not there, the station manager adds it on the **Schedule** tab.

## Join off air near the handover

Ask the presenter on air for an invitation with the role **DJ**.
Open it in a browser and press **Join the studio**: a DJ arrives off air, under **Not on air**, and listeners hear nothing of you.
Wait there until the presenter hands the air to you ([Hand over to the next DJ](/guides/hand-over/)).

## When the air is yours

Press **Switch to Live** in the band at the top of the desk.
If someone else's show is still on air, the app asks first: switch only when you are taking over the desk.

## If it does not work

- **The desk's buttons are greyed out.** The window is preparing: press **Switch to Live** when the desk is yours.
- **The station does not move to your show at the post.** It is not scheduled, or its time is wrong: check it on the **Schedule** tab.
- **You went on air the moment you joined.** The invitation was for a guest or a presenter: leave, and ask for one with the role **DJ**.
