When you finish, you will be on air with the presenter, heard by the listeners.

## Open the link

1. Open the invitation link the station sent you, in a web browser.
2. Press **Join the studio**, and allow the microphone when the browser asks.

A guest is on air the moment they join, so listeners hear you as soon as you have joined.

## Wear headphones

Wear headphones, never speakers, so your speakers do not send the presenter's voice back on air as an echo.
Closed-back headphones at a comfortable level leak least.

Keep the microphone close to your mouth, and away from the headphones' ear cups.

## If it does not work

- **The page says your microphone is blocked.** Allow the microphone for the page, by the address, then press **Try again**.
- **The presenter hears an echo of their own voice.** Your speakers are reaching your microphone: put on headphones, and the echo goes.
