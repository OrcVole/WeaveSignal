When you finish, you will have presented a show: on air, heard clearly, and able to put people on air and off from the desk.

You use two windows: the **studio app**, which shows the desk and takes your keys, and a **browser page**, which carries your voice.

## Get ready

1. Put on headphones, never speakers, so nobody hears themselves echo back on air.
2. Open the studio app and sign in. It opens on the **Desk** tab.
3. Join with your voice:
   - press **Join with my microphone…** on the empty stage, then **Open in browser**;
   - or open the invitation link the station sent you, in a browser.
4. On the page, press **Join the studio**, and allow the microphone when the browser asks.

As a presenter you are on air from the moment you join, in the first slot of the desk.
Keep the microphone close to your mouth.

## Read the desk

The desk is the first tab of the studio app.
At its top, the **lamp** answers whether listeners can hear the room.
The **stage** holds everyone on air, as tiles in their slots.
Under it, **Not on air** holds everyone in the room who is not on air yet.

## Put someone on air, and off

Guests join with their own link and are on air the moment they arrive.
Press their slot's number, `1` to `5`, to put someone on air, or the same key to take them off.

## Hear someone before air, and mute them

Press a slot's cue key, `Q` `W` `E` `R` `T`, to hear a person in your headphones only, before you put them on air.
Press their mute key, `Z` `X` `C` `V` `B`, to cut them from the programme while they stay on the stage.

## End the show

Press `⌃⇧E` to end the show, and confirm when it asks.

## If it does not work

- **There is an echo on air.** Someone is using speakers: ask everyone to wear headphones ([why](/docs/explanation/echo-and-headphones/)).
- **There is no Join with my microphone… button.** Someone is already presenting, this window is preparing a show, or your account is not this show's presenter: use the invitation link the station sent you.
- **The keys do nothing.** You are typing in a field: press `Esc` to leave it, and the desk keys work again.
