When you finish, you will know what the desk's words mean, and what to do when listeners stop hearing you or there is an echo.

## The words the desk shows

The desk puts one word on every tile and the lamp, so nothing depends on colour:

- **ON AIR** — listeners can hear this now; speak as if the whole audience is in the room.
- **OPEN** — the people in the room hear this person, but listeners do not, because the station is not taking the desk just now.
- **CUE** — this person is in your headphones only: use it to check someone before you put them on air.
- **MUTED** — this person is cut from everything; nobody hears them until you unmute them.
- **STANDBY** — nobody is on air, and the station is playing its music; the next On key puts you on air at once.

## If listeners stop hearing the room

If the lamp says **PLAYOUT DOWN**, the part of the station that sends your show to listeners has stopped.
Everyone in the room still hears each other, and your tiles say OPEN; listeners hear the station's standby music.

1. Tell the people in the room, plainly.
2. Tell whoever looks after the station: they restart it.
3. Carry on. When it is back, the lamp and your tiles say ON AIR again by themselves.

## If a guest echoes on air

An echo on air almost always means someone is using speakers: ask everyone to wear headphones ([why](/docs/explanation/echo-and-headphones/)).

## If it does not work

- **The lamp says FAULT.** The desk itself has stopped. It usually starts again by itself within a minute; if not, tell whoever looks after the station.
- **PLAYOUT DOWN does not go away after a restart.** Whoever looks after the station should follow [When the lamp says PLAYOUT DOWN](/docs/how-to/playout-down/): it names the rare fix.
