These are the studio words you are most likely to meet, in the sense they carry here.

- **The top bar, the left panel, the right panel.** The studio app's places: the top bar runs along the top (the step, the tabs, Settings, who you are, the text size); on the Desk tab the left panel holds the clock and what comes up next, the stage in the middle holds the people, and the right panel holds listeners, the picture, the carts and the chat.
- **The desk.** The mixing desk as software: it hears every person in the room, mixes the programme, and gives each of them a mix of their own.
- **Mix-minus.** The mix a person hears: everyone they should hear, never their own voice, so nobody hears themselves back late.
- **The programme.** What listeners hear: the desk's mix of everyone on air, then the stream.
- **Playout.** The part of the station that plays music and recorded shows between live shows, and passes the live show on to listeners.
- **Cue.** Someone in your headphones only: cue a caller to check them before you put them on air; listeners hear nothing of it.
- **Talkback.** Your voice to one person, off air, for as long as you hold the key: a word to a guest, a count-in, a caller told they are next.
- **On, off, muted.** *On* puts a source on air; *off* takes them off; *muted* cuts them from every mix.
- **A cart.** A short sound on a key: a jingle, a sting, applause.
- **The rundown.** The running order of a show: what comes next and when, shown beside the desk.
- **The profanity delay, the dump.** Listeners hear the programme a few seconds after the room; the dump throws that held stretch away, so listeners never hear it.
- **The lamp.** The big word at the top of the desk that says whether listeners can hear the room, such as ON AIR or PLAYOUT DOWN.
- **The slate.** The picture shown when video is on and nobody is on camera.
- **A bed.** A cart that plays under speech, ducked beneath the voice.
- **Count-in.** The producer's talkback before a show starts, so a remote guest knows when to speak.
- **The screener.** The person who answers callers off air, talks to them, and sends one to the desk.

## If it does not work

- **You meet a word that is not on this list.** The [glossary](/docs/reference/glossary/) lists every studio word, in the full documentation.
