When you finish, you will know why there is no sound, and how to get it.
Find what you see below; each answer takes a minute or two.

## Nothing at all comes from the studio app

That is how it is made.
The studio app shows the desk and sends your keys; it plays nothing of the station, and it does not carry your voice.
Sound goes through a browser on the same computer:

- to **hear the station**, open its public address and press **Listen live**;
- to **speak and hear the studio**, press **Join with my microphone…** on the Desk tab, then **Open in browser**, and keep that tab open.

## The player is silent

1. Check the tab is not muted, and that the computer plays sound from other sites.
2. Wait half a minute: listeners are some seconds behind the studio, and the desk's clock says how far.
3. On the Desk tab, read the lamp. **AUTO** means automation holds the air: press **Take the air back** if you want the desk heard. **PLAYOUT DOWN** means listeners hear the standby sound until playout is back.

## You do not hear the studio in your headphones

1. Check your microphone link's tab is still open, and joined.
2. Remember that nobody hears their own voice from the studio. Ask someone else to speak, or fire a cart.
3. Choose your headphones as the browser's output ([how](/docs/how-to/choose-your-microphone-and-speakers/)).

## Nobody hears you

1. If the page says **Your microphone is blocked**, allow it by the lock at the left of the address, then press **Try again**.
2. On the Desk tab, look at your tile: it must say **ON AIR** to reach listeners. You join standing by; your slot's key, `1`, puts you on air.
3. If your tile's meter does not move when you speak, the browser has another microphone: [choose yours](/docs/how-to/choose-your-microphone-and-speakers/).

## A cart plays nowhere

1. A window that says **Preparing** fires nothing: use a window that is not preparing a show.
2. A cart in a show's own bank plays only while that show is on air: use a bank marked **station**.
3. A pad that says **FILE MISSING** has lost its sound: edit it and choose the sound again.

## If it does not work

- **The echo test is silent too** (your public address followed by `/echo-test/`): the browser and Windows are using other devices. [Choose your microphone and speakers](/docs/how-to/choose-your-microphone-and-speakers/).
- **The player says the stream stopped**: tell whoever looks after the station; the station's listener stream is down, or asks for a password the player cannot give.
