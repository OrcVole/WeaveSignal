When you finish, the next DJ holds the air alone, with no gap between your shows.

## Before your show ends

Both shows are in the schedule back to back, each with its own presenter's name and takedown contact, and each of you has a staff account to sign in with.
Agree, before the air, when the next DJ joins the room, when they come on air, and who says what in the overlap.

## Bring the next DJ in early

1. On the Desk tab, press **Invite someone…** at the right of the **Not on air** line.
2. Type their name, keep **DJ** under **They join as**, and press **Create invitation**, then **Copy link**.
3. Send the link to them: a DJ joins off air, under **Not on air**, and nothing of them reaches listeners until you put them on.

Hear their level with their cue key, and agree the plan off air with their talk key, the same as any guest.

## Hand over

1. Announce your last link, and say who is next.
2. Cue the next DJ, hold their talk key, and count them in: "In three, two, one."
3. Press `⇧⏎`: the next DJ is on air beside you, and both tiles say ON AIR.
   Say hello, let them say what is in their show, say goodbye.
4. Press `⇧⏎` again: you are off air, and they hold the air alone.
   They press **Switch to Live** in their own studio app, so their keys drive the desk.
5. Leave the room; the desk keeps your strip for a moment, then frees it.

The button under the next DJ's tiles does the same as these keys, naming what the next press does.

## If it does not work

- **The next DJ is not under Not on air.** They have not joined: carry on presenting and talk to them by phone, and do not take yourself off until they are on air.
- **You take yourself off before they are on air.** Listeners hear nobody until the next DJ is on: the `⇧⏎` keys take you off only once they are already on air.
