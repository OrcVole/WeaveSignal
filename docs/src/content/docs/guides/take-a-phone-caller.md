When you finish, you will have put a listener's call on air, and taken it off again.

## When a screener sends you a caller

A caller rings in from the station's player page, and agrees to be recorded.
The screener talks to them first, then sends one to you.
The toast says *⟨name⟩ is ready for you*, with the screener's line under it.

1. Press `R` to take the caller: you hear them in your headphones only.
2. Hold `F` to talk to them off air, if you need to.
3. Press `4` to put them on air.
4. Press `4` again to take them off at the end of the call.

If you are not ready, press `Esc` (*Not now*): the caller stays ready, and the screener can send them again.

## When you screen the callers yourself

With no screener, the callers wait in **Callers** on the right of your desk.
Press `P`, or **Take … to strip 4**: the caller who has waited longest joins you, off air, and the steps above are the same.

## If it does not work

- **The caller says the studio is too quiet.** On a phone, a call has its own volume, so ask them to press volume up once the call has started.
- **The caller's sound breaks up.** That comes from their network, not your desk; ask them to move to a better connection.
