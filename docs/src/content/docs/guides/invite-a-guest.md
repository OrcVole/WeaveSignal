When you finish, you will have sent a link that puts a guest on air beside you.

## Invite them

1. On the Desk tab, press **Invite someone…** at the right of the **Not on air** line.
2. Type their name, and keep **Guest** under **They join as**.
3. Press **Create invitation**, then **Copy link**.

A guest is on air the moment they join, so this is the role for anyone who talks to your listeners.
An invitation is a link that works for a while and for anyone who has it, so send it to the guest alone.

## What they do with it

The guest opens the link in a browser, presses **Join the studio**, and allows the microphone.
They wear headphones, the same as you, so they do not echo back on air.

They are on air the moment they join, in their own slot on your desk.

## If it does not work

- **The guest is not on your desk.** They have not pressed **Join the studio** yet: a guest is on air the moment they join.
- **The page says their microphone is blocked.** They allow the microphone for the page, then press **Try again** ([get ready](/docs/how-to/get-ready/)).
