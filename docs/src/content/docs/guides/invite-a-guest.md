When you finish, you will have sent a link that puts a guest on air beside you.

## Invite them

1. On the Desk tab, press **Invite someone…** at the right of the **Not on air** line.
2. Type their name, and keep **Guest** under **They join as**.
3. Press **Create invitation**, then **Copy link**.

A guest joins standing by, off air, until you put them on: this is the role for anyone who talks to your listeners.
An invitation is a link that works for a while and for anyone who has it, so send it to the guest alone.

## What they do with it

The guest opens the link in a browser, presses **Join the studio**, and allows the microphone.
They wear headphones, the same as you, so they do not echo back on air.

They wait under **Not on air**, in their own slot on your desk. Press their slot's digit to put them on air, and again to take them off.

## If it does not work

- **The guest is not on your desk.** They have not pressed **Join the studio** yet. Once they have, they wait under **Not on air** until you put them on.
- **The page says their microphone is blocked.** They allow the microphone for the page, then press **Try again** ([get ready](/docs/how-to/get-ready/)).
- **You hear the wrong microphone, or their tile's meter does not move.** On their page, under **Your microphone**, they choose the right one and press **Use** with its name. They stay in the room ([more](/docs/how-to/choose-your-microphone-and-speakers/)).
- **They opened the page without its link** and read **Join with an invitation**: they need the whole link you sent. Send it again.
