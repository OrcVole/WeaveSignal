Spike 01: echo test

Wear headphones. Press Join and allow the microphone. You will hear the desk's mix-minus for you: participant A's clicks, and anyone else in the room, but never your own voice. Talk for a minute and listen for any trace of yourself coming back (echo), a hollow or phasey sound, or a lag on the clicks.

The browser's own echo cancelling is switched off on purpose, so anything you hear of yourself is the desk's fault, not hidden by the browser.
